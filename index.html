My Projects & Case Studies

PRIMA (Printer Inventory & Maintenance Application)
PT Angkasa Pura Indonesia — Airport Technology Services Division

PRIMA is a web-based information system developed to digitize printer inventory monitoring, toner/ribbon consumption, and preventive maintenance for Managed Print Services at PT Angkasa Pura Indonesia. It replaces manual tracking to streamline operational reporting and ensure accurate third-party vendor billing verification.

ETAMUGFOREST
PT Kaniki Parama Abadi

ETAMUGFOREST is a web-based guest registration and client directory system developed for PT Kaniki Parama Abadi to digitize event participant check-ins via QR Code and centralize client collaboration records. It replaces manual spreadsheet tracking with real-time attendance monitoring and automated analytics to support corporate event operations.

Business Process Analysis & Re-Engineering
PT Hanja Prima Loka

PT Hanja Prima Loka is a business process analysis and re-engineering case study focused on optimizing gallon manufacturing and supply chain workflows using BPMN in Bizagi Modeler. By applying Value-Added Analysis to eliminate redundant Non-Value-Added (NVA) administrative steps and translating inventory bottlenecks into structured functional requirements, it establishes a streamlined TO-BE workflow for production and stock operations.

SPORTIVE
Mobile App UI/UX Prototype & Usability Testing

Sportive is a mobile application UI/UX prototype designed to streamline sports venue bookings, sparring matchmaking, and running target tracking for local sports enthusiasts. Validated through scenario-based Usability Testing with 5 participants and synthesized via a Feedback Grid, the project established an actionable design iteration plan covering dark mode themes, color contrast improvements, and online transportation integration.
